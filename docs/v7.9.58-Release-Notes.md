## Release Overview

The lock screen set was pruned from 88 images to 32 and fully
regenerated. Every image now follows one of two intentional
themes — a blowfish mark, or a futuristic cityscape with a
pufferfish inkblot woven into the skyline. Lime green accents,
ornate borders, and a crisp "Locked" over "Enter your password"
readout that actually invites the password prompt instead of
hiding from it.

**Total changes: 88 images consolidated to 32, all regenerated.**

---

## 🔒 Lock Screens: Curated, Not Accumulated

The old set had grown to 88 images of wildly inconsistent
quality. Locking the machine was a lottery. The new set is 32
images, every one sharing a common visual language:

- **Two themes.** A blowfish mark, or a continuous futuristic
  cityscape with an OpenBSD pufferfish rendered as an inkblot
  inside the skyline. No other variations. No filler.
- **Lime green splashes.** The OpenRiot accent color runs
  through every frame as the binding thread.
- **Readable lock text.** "Locked" sits large and crisp over
  "Enter your password." No password boxes, no fake UI, no
  menus or taskbars or dates cluttering the frame.
- **Ornate borders.** Each image is framed, edge to edge.
- **Subtle branding.** "OpenRiot" appears in the background,
  present but never loud.

The lock screen now looks like a product decision instead of
a screenshot folder that got out of hand.
