> _"In a city of eternal rain, every reflection is a memory."_

## Release Overview

Sixteen new backgrounds, drawn from the wet pavement and neon of
Bladerunner's futures and the rain-lashed, mud-spattered feudal
epics of Akira Kurosawa. Rōnin cross the street with replicants;
swords and umbrellas share the frame with flying cars. From
**27.webp** through **42.webp**, the collection now spans 42
wallpapers — a full deck.
