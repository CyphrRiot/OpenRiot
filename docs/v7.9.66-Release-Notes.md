## Release Overview

The Control Center is one keyboard-driven panel that holds six
sections of the system: audio, display, network, power, privacy,
and system. Arrow keys move, enter acts, `+`/`-` adjust. No more
memorizing which key turns down the volume or which chord toggles
the tunnel. It ships Beta — the construct keeps adding rooms.

While wiring it, we caught the bar lying about HDMI and
brightness. Both are fixed.

**Total changes: `--control-center` command, a polybar icon, a
launcher entry, a keybinding, and two display-truth fixes.**

---

## 🎛️ The Six Rooms

The Control Center is a TUI, the same keyboard-first form as
`--nmtui` and `--disk`. It opens floating and centered, sized to
what it holds.

| Section | Controls |
|---------|----------|
| Audio | Volume bar, mute |
| Display | Brightness, HDMI toggle, resolution |
| Network | WiFi, Ethernet, WireGuard |
| Power | Battery, suspend, reboot, shutdown, lock |
| Privacy | Night light, stealth, Proton sync |
| System | Updates, screen recording, screenshot |

`↑`/`↓` walk the items, `←`/`→` walk the sections, `enter` acts,
`+`/`-` adjust volume or brightness. Three ways in:

- `Super + Ctrl + Space`
- the 󰐰 faders icon on the bar
- Control Center in the launcher

Every action is the same code the bar and the menus already ran —
the Control Center just gathers the switches into one room instead
of leaving them scattered across the sprawl.

## 🕯️ The Bar Was Lying

Two indicators were telling stories that weren't true, and we
found them while wiring the panel.

The HDMI icon said "not connected" while the monitor was lit. The
bar was asking i3 which outputs were active, and i3 had quietly
dropped HDMI-1 from its list even though xrandr was still driving
it at 1920x1080. The construct now asks xrandr directly — the
source that tells the truth about what is plugged in and what is
lit.

The brightness read zero while the screen was plainly on. The
backlight tool answers in decimals, `5.001805`, and the old code
only understood whole numbers, so it threw the answer away and
reported nothing. That is fixed at the root, and the brightness
keys and the Control Center now share one honest reading.

## 🗺️ One More Door

Proton Mail moved into the Office menu, where it belongs next to
the word processor and the spreadsheet. The Control Center took
the bottom slot in the launcher, under Utilities, wearing the
faders icon on the bar. The beta panel is documented in the
README with its sections and keys.
